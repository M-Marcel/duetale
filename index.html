Where most people are

Published rates

What Nigeria charges

These are the personal income tax bands under the 2025 Acts. They apply to your worldwide income as a Nigerian resident, foreign earnings included. Most people we speak to have never seen them laid out.

₦0 – ₦800,000 0%
₦800,001 – ₦3,000,000 15%
₦3,000,001 – ₦12,000,000 18%
₦12,000,001 – ₦25,000,000 21%
₦25,000,001 – ₦50,000,000 23%
Above ₦50,000,000 25%

Bands apply progressively — each slice of income is taxed at its own rate, not the whole amount at the top rate. Reliefs and allowances change the final figure. This is published information, not advice on your situation.
