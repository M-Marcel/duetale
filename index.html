Three positions

Almost everyone earning well in Nigeria — in naira or foreign currency — is in one of three positions. None of them is unusual, and none of them is a disaster.

  • Not registered

    You've never registered for tax here, and you're not sure whether you were supposed to.

  • Registered, not filing

    You have a TIN somewhere from a job years ago, and nothing has been filed since.

  • Filing, unsure

    Something gets filed each year. You couldn't say with confidence whether it's right.

What none of them tell you is what it would actually cost to fix — which is the thing worth knowing before you decide anything.

What Nigeria charges

These are the personal income tax bands under the 2025 Acts. They apply to your worldwide income as a Nigerian resident, foreign earnings included. Most people we speak to have never seen them laid out.

₦0 – ₦800,000 0%
₦800,001 – ₦3,000,000 15%
₦3,000,001 – ₦12,000,000 18%
₦12,000,001 – ₦25,000,000 21%
₦25,000,001 – ₦50,000,000 23%
Above ₦50,000,000 25%

Bands apply progressively — each slice of income is taxed at its own rate, not the whole amount at the top rate. Reliefs and allowances change the final figure. This is published information, not advice on your situation.

How this works, structurally

  • Duetale does not file your taxes.

    A licensed CITN practitioner does. In Nigeria that is a legal requirement, not a technical one, and software cannot replace it.

  • Duetale does not hold your money.

    Tax payments go directly to the authority against a payment reference. Only our service fee ever touches our account.

  • Duetale is software and coordination.

    We handle the records, the computation, the chasing and the paperwork. The professional judgment belongs to professionals.
